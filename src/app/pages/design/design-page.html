The Little Surprises design system

Every colour, type size, control, card and section the shop is built from, live and working. Change the appearance in the bar above to see each one in dark mode.

Content first. The photograph and the product carry the page; the chrome around them stays quiet.

One accent. Wine is for actions. Rose is the ribbon, and never the colour of small text.

Tone, not lines. Surfaces part with a step in colour, never a border or a resting shadow.

The tag is the signature. The “For you” tag from the logo carries the customer's own words.
